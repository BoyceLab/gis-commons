# Communities to join

Open standards are maintained by working groups that welcome new contributors, including people who bring a use case and no code. This page lists where to take a question or a proposal.

| Community | What to bring | How to take part |
|---|---|---|
| OHDSI GIS working group | A research question that needs geographic or environmental data joined to OMOP | Weekly meetings on Fridays at 10 a.m. Eastern; propose a use case as a GitHub issue. [Get started](https://ohdsi.github.io/GIS/get-started.html) |
| ECTO | A request for a new exposure class or a route or medium variant | Open an issue on the [ECTO tracker](https://github.com/EnvironmentOntology/environmental-exposure-ontology/issues) |
| ENVO | A missing environmental feature, material or process | Open an issue on the [ENVO tracker](https://github.com/EnvironmentOntology/envo/issues) |
| Mondo | Disease term requests and mapping corrections | [Mondo tracker](https://github.com/monarch-initiative/mondo/issues) |
| HPO | Phenotype term requests | [HPO tracker](https://github.com/obophenotype/human-phenotype-ontology/issues) |
| Monarch Initiative | Cross-ontology questions spanning disease, phenotype and exposure | [monarchinitiative.org](https://monarchinitiative.org/) |
| GA4GH | Proposals for Phenopackets and Beacon | [ga4gh.org](https://www.ga4gh.org/) |
| LinkML | Schema design questions | [linkml.io](https://linkml.io/) |
| LOINC | Requests for new observation codes | [loinc.org/submissions](https://loinc.org/submissions/) |
| Open Geospatial Consortium (OGC) | Geospatial interoperability, including its health domain working group | [ogc.org](https://www.ogc.org/) |
| Esri Community | ArcGIS questions, including a health and human services space | [community.esri.com](https://community.esri.com/) |

## Writing a useful term request

A request is most likely to be accepted when it includes a proposed label, a one-sentence definition with a citation, the parent term, the design pattern if the ontology uses them, and the use case that needs it. Search labels and synonyms first, because a concept may exist under a different preferred name.
