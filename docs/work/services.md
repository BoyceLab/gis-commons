# Work with our experts

Bring us a question, a data challenge or a story that needs a clearer way to reach people. A project can involve advice, a jointly developed product, a defined service, training or a longer research partnership.

## Expertise and project ideas

| Area | Examples of work | A useful first deliverable |
|---|---|---|
| GIS and environmental epidemiology | Residential histories, environmental linkage, access to care, spatial study design and interpretation | A study design and data feasibility plan |
| Real world evidence and electronic health records | Cohort definitions, record completeness, longitudinal histories, data dictionaries and registry linkage | A cohort specification and data quality assessment |
| AI and informatics | Reviewing AI extraction from narratives, terminology mapping, human review workflows and validation | An evaluation plan with error categories and a reviewed example set |
| Data visualization | Dashboards, figures, interactive explanations and accessible summaries | A prototype matched to a defined audience |
| Web design for research and advocacy | Resource hubs, educational tools, project websites and accessible navigation | A content plan and working prototype |
| StoryMaps and storytelling | Story planning, writing, media selection, interactive design and publication support | A storyboard or a completed story, depending on scope |
| Training and mentoring | Workshops, short courses, office hours and train-the-trainer sessions | Learning objectives, an agenda and a practice exercise |

For research involving sensitive information, define data access, agreements and review requirements before transferring records. AI-assisted findings require validation against source material; a generated summary is not a verified research result.

## Tell a story with StoryMaps

A StoryMap is an interactive web narrative that can bring together text, photographs, video, audio, charts and other media. Maps can be embedded when they help explain the story; a story does not have to center on a map. Esri's [ArcGIS StoryMaps overview](https://www.esri.com/en-us/arcgis/products/arcgis-storymaps/overview) shows the format, and our [StoryMaps gallery](../maps/storymaps.md) includes ALS examples.

We can advise your organization or help create the story with you. Examples include an organization's history, a person's experience shared with permission, barriers to care, a research project, a community initiative or a public explanation of findings.

### Ways we can help

- **Story consultation:** clarify the audience, purpose, key message and the material you already have.
- **Storyboard and editorial support:** organize the sequence, write or refine the narrative, and identify where media or interaction adds useful context.
- **Story creation:** assemble the agreed text, maps, images and media into an interactive page, including source credits and accessibility checks.
- **Review and training:** review an existing story or teach your team to build and maintain one.

### What to bring

Describe the audience, what you want readers to understand or do, the deadline and budget range, and the material available. For stories about people, identify the permissions needed for names, quotations, images, audio and video. Agree on who owns the content, which account hosts the story, review responsibilities and maintenance after publication.

Possible deliverables are an outline, a storyboard, a draft for review, a published story under an agreed hosting arrangement and a short maintenance guide. Publication and any public use of a person's story require their separate permission or another approved basis; research participation alone is not permission to publish an identifiable story.
