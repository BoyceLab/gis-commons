# Work and volunteer with GeOALS

GeOALS brings together geographic analysis, research methods and communication to support the ALS community. Our expertise extends beyond maps to real world evidence, electronic health records, environmental epidemiology, AI, visualization and web design.

You can bring a research question, a story to tell, a skill to share or a few hours to help. Start with an activity below and agree on a manageable first task with us.

## Start a conversation

Email [Danielle Boyce](mailto:danielle@boycedatascience.com) with your organization or interests, what you hope to accomplish, the skills or support you need, your timing and an approximate budget or volunteer time commitment. A short description is enough to begin. Please do not send medical records, participant details, home addresses or private data in an introductory email.

We can help define the question, suggest a first deliverable and identify the expertise required. Paid project work, volunteering and participation in a research study are separate activities. Volunteering is not required to receive information about a study.
