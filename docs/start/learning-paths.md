# Learning paths by role

Each path lists resources in the order a newcomer would use them. Nearly all are free. Esri courses are described further on [ArcGIS tools and training](../esri/tools.md).

## Advocates and people living with ALS

The goal is to use existing maps with confidence and to bring one to a conversation with a legislator or a clinic.

- Watch the [ALS Geospatial Hub Webinar](../maps/videos.md#v-7jOmK_Df6so) for an overview of the maps for care and for advocacy.
- Use the [Glen Rouse ALS Legislative Viewer](../maps/tools.md#glen-rouse-als-legislative-viewer) to look up your legislators, then watch the [Legislative Viewer 2.0 walk-through](../maps/videos.md#v-nBxY4QUmHhY).
- Review your clinic with the [ALS Clinic Advisor survey](../maps/tools.md#als-clinic-advisor), which is designed to work with eye-gaze devices.
- Read [Reading a map critically](gis-basics.md#reading-a-map-critically).
- Explore the [ALS StoryMaps examples](../maps/storymaps.md), then use [Esri's StoryMaps overview and training resources](../esri/tools.md#learn-arcgis-storymaps) to plan and build a story of your own.

## Clinic teams and care organizations

The goal is to understand a clinic's catchment and how it compares with need.

- Explore the [ALS Clinic Advisor Locator and Dashboard](../maps/tools.md#als-clinic-advisor).
- Read the Esri article [Using GIS to Elevate Clinic Quality and Improve Care for ALS / MND Patients](https://www.esri.com/en-us/industries/blog/articles/using-gis-to-elevate-clinic-quality-and-improve-care-for-als-mnd-patients).
- Take Esri's free [GIS Basics](https://www.esri.com/training/catalog/5d9cd7de5edc347a71611ccc/gis-basics/) and [ArcGIS Online Basics](https://www.esri.com/training/catalog/5d816c0255cf937306d2d3ef/arcgis-online-basics/) web courses.
- Review the [privacy page](../governance/privacy.md) before mapping any patient addresses.

## Researchers new to mapping

The goal is to design a study with a geographic component and to know what to ask a GIS partner for.

- Read [GIS in plain language](gis-basics.md) and the [glossary](glossary.md).
- Watch [Mapping the CDC National ALS Registry](../maps/videos.md#v-1vZd74qdI68) as a worked example of registry data placed on a map.
- Work through Esri's free health tutorials, introduced in the Esri Community post [An ArcGIS tutorial series for developing essential GIS skills for health workflows](https://community.esri.com/discussion/1241346/presenting-health-gis-curriculum).
- Read [The standards stack](../standards/overview.md) to plan how location will be stored.
- Use the [Grant toolkit](../grants/index.md) when the study moves to a proposal.

## Data scientists and informaticians

The goal is reproducible spatial analysis and standards-based storage.

- [Geocomputation with R](https://r.geocompx.org/) or [Geocomputation with Python](https://py.geocompx.org/).
- [Spatial Data Science: With Applications in R](https://r-spatial.org/book/).
- The [OHDSI GIS working group documentation](https://ohdsi.github.io/GIS/), including its [schema extensions](https://ohdsi.github.io/GIS/schema-extensions.html) and [getting started page](https://ohdsi.github.io/GIS/get-started.html).
- The [ArcGIS API for Python](https://developers.arcgis.com/python/) and the [R-ArcGIS Bridge](https://r.esri.com/) for working with hosted ArcGIS layers from code.
- [Geospatial data standards](../standards/overview.md#geospatial-data-formats-and-identifiers) on this site.

## Training for your team

GeoALS experts can tailor a workshop, short course or mentoring session to your group's goals. Topics include StoryMaps, GIS fundamentals, web maps and dashboards, environmental linkage, data standards and location privacy. See [Training with GeoALS](../grants/training.md) for the training menu and an example project.

## Open source tools

GeoALS builds on ArcGIS. Partners who work in open source tools can exchange data with us through open formats.

| Tool | Use | Link |
|---|---|---|
| QGIS | Desktop GIS | [qgis.org](https://qgis.org/) |
| PostGIS | Spatial database on PostgreSQL | [postgis.net](https://postgis.net/) |
| GDAL | Format conversion and raster processing | [gdal.org](https://gdal.org/) |
| sf, terra, tidycensus | Spatial analysis and census data in R | [r-spatial.org](https://r-spatial.org/) |
| GeoPandas | Spatial analysis in Python | [geopandas.org](https://geopandas.org/) |
| Leaflet and MapLibre | Web maps | [leafletjs.com](https://leafletjs.com/), [maplibre.org](https://maplibre.org/) |
| DeGAUSS | Geocoding and geographic variable assignment that runs locally, so addresses stay inside the institution | [degauss.org](https://degauss.org/) |
| Census Geocoder | Free address geocoding for United States addresses | [geocoding.geo.census.gov](https://geocoding.geo.census.gov/) |
