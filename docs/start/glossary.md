# Glossary

ALS
:   Amyotrophic lateral sclerosis, a progressive neurodegenerative disease of motor neurons. Called motor neuron disease (MND) in many countries.

ArcGIS
:   Esri's family of GIS software, including ArcGIS Online, ArcGIS Pro and the app builders described on [ArcGIS tools and training](../esri/tools.md).

Buffer
:   The area within a chosen distance of a feature.

Census tract
:   A small statistical area defined by the Census Bureau, generally holding a few thousand residents.

Choropleth
:   A map that shades areas according to a value. Suited to rates, and misleading for raw counts.

Coordinate reference system (CRS)
:   The definition that gives coordinates their meaning on the earth.

ECTO
:   The Environmental Conditions, Treatments and Exposures Ontology, an open ontology for describing exposure events.

Exposome
:   The totality of environmental exposures across a person's life.

FAIR
:   Findable, accessible, interoperable and reusable: principles for managing research data.

Feature layer
:   A hosted set of geographic features with attributes, the basic building block of ArcGIS web maps.

Geocoding
:   Converting an address to coordinates.

GEOID
:   The Census Bureau identifier for a geographic area, built from Federal Information Processing Series (FIPS) codes.

GIS
:   Geographic information system.

HIPAA
:   The Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act, the United States law whose Privacy Rule lists geographic identifiers among the elements that make health information identifiable.

IRB
:   Institutional review board, the committee that reviews research involving human participants.

Mappers
:   The volunteer group of GIS professionals, ALS researchers and advocates who build GeoALS maps and apps.

MND
:   Motor neuron disease.

OHDSI
:   Observational Health Data Sciences and Informatics, the open science community that maintains the OMOP Common Data Model.

OMOP CDM
:   The Observational Medical Outcomes Partnership Common Data Model, a standard structure for observational health data.

Ontology
:   A structured vocabulary in which each concept has an identifier, a definition, and stated relationships to other concepts.

Raster
:   Gridded data in which each cell holds a value.

Residential history
:   The sequence of addresses where a person has lived, with dates.

Service area
:   The region reachable from a location within a set travel time or distance.

Spatial join
:   Attaching attributes from one layer to another on the basis of location.

Vector
:   Geographic data stored as points, lines or polygons.

ZCTA
:   ZIP Code Tabulation Area, the Census Bureau's areal approximation of a ZIP Code.
