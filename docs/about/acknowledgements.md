# Acknowledgements and sources

GIS Commons was developed by Danielle Boyce, a member of the board at GeoALS. The sources below inform the site; linked maps, videos and stories retain their original authorship and credits.

## GeoALS

Pat Dolan founded GeoALS and the ALS Geospatial Hub. GeoALS maps are built by the Mappers, a volunteer group of GIS professionals, ALS researchers and advocates, listed on the [ALS Geospatial Hub](https://als-geospatial-hub-nonprofit.hub.arcgis.com/pages/the-people). To learn about GeoALS, its history and its people, visit the [GeoALS website](https://www.geoals.org/).
