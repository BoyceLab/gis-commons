# Suggest a resource or correction

To suggest a map, StoryMap, training resource, research reference or correction, email [Danielle Boyce](mailto:danielle@boycedatascience.com).

Include:

- The page title or link
- What you suggest adding or changing
- A link to the original source, with its publication or update date when available
- A short explanation of how the resource helps the ALS or mapping community

Please leave out participant information, home addresses and confidential study material. Contributions are reviewed for relevance, clarity and source support before they are added.
