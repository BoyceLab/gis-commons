# Program plan

A prototype plan for discussion with the GeoALS board, prepared 12 November 2026. It describes what this site could accomplish for GeoALS, the programs it would support, and how progress could be measured. Nothing here is a GeoALS commitment.

## Purpose of the site

GeoALS's public apps show what the organization has built. This site explains how the work is done and how others can join it, which serves several aims at once:

- **Continuity.** Documenting how the maps are made helps new Mappers contribute sooner and keeps the work going.
- **Research partnerships.** Investigators can see what GeoALS offers and find the language to include it in a proposal, which opens a path to grant and contract revenue.
- **Esri tools.** The site documents the ArcGIS products the maps use and links to free Esri training.
- **Field leadership.** A public reference on mapping, standards and environmental context for neurodegenerative disease could make GeoALS a starting point for ALS and for neighboring disease communities.
- **Training.** Advocacy organizations and clinic teams gain a guided route into mapping.

## What the site contains now

- An orientation to GIS, role-based learning paths and a glossary
- A directory of GeoALS apps and a gallery of every video on the ALS Geospatial Hub YouTube channel, with descriptions, refreshed by a scheduled workflow
- Guidance on crediting maps and software, and a directory of ArcGIS products and free training
- An overview of standards and ontologies for location and environmental context, with the communities that maintain them
- Six environmental exposure examples with study designs, data source links, primary references and workbook provenance
- A grant toolkit: partnership arrangements, starter ideas, proposal language, budget justification and letter templates, and a request checklist
- Privacy and governance practices
- Repository automation: site build and deployment, link checking, and issue templates for feedback and resource suggestions

## Planned additions

- Reproducible environmental linkage tutorials using public or synthetic data, building on the exposure examples
- Per-exposure standards coverage tables from the manuscript in preparation, once it is submitted
- Worked tutorials that reproduce a GeoALS map from public data
- A case study page for each research collaboration, written with the partner
- A page for other disease communities on adapting the clinic locator and legislative viewer
- Translations of the orientation pages

## Measures of progress

| Aim | Possible measure |
|---|---|
| Research partnerships | Partnership requests received; proposals submitted with GeoALS named; awards |
| Training | Site visits to the learning paths; workshop attendance |
| Open science | Term requests and use cases submitted; data layers published with full metadata |
| Community | Clinic reviews submitted; clinics added; active Mappers |
| Sustainability | Share of operating costs covered by grants, contracts and donations |

## Decisions for the board

- Adopt, revise or decline the partnership arrangements in the grant toolkit
- Set an indirect cost rate and consultant and service rates
- Decide how GeoALS products acknowledge Esri
- Choose licenses for content, code and data
- Decide whether GeoALS adopts this prototype, and where an adopted version would live
- Name who reviews partnership requests and signs letters
- Confirm the small-cell suppression threshold and the privacy practices as policy

The specific placeholders are listed on [Items to confirm](confirm.md).
