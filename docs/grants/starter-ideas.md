# Grant starter ideas

Project ideas with a geographic component, grouped by the kind of funder they suit. Each lists the role GeoALS would play. They are starting points to adapt to a funder's priorities. Confirm interest, capacity and scope with GeoALS before including a role in a proposal.

## Access to care

| Idea | GeoALS role | Suits |
|---|---|---|
| Measure travel time to multidisciplinary clinics and identify communities far from care | Drive-time analysis, maps, public dashboard | Foundations focused on care; health services research programs |
| Pair clinic review data with location to study variation in services offered | Clinic Advisor data, analysis support | Quality improvement funders |
| Plan satellite or telehealth-supported clinic sites using population and travel data | Site suitability analysis | Health systems, state programs |
| Map durable medical equipment loan closets, support groups and respite services against need | Data collection through survey apps, mapping | Community foundations |

## Research recruitment and registries

| Idea | GeoALS role | Suits |
|---|---|---|
| Compare where study participants live with where people with the disease are expected to live, to guide outreach | Recruitment pattern analysis, as done with partner studies | Any study with a recruitment aim |
| Add a geographic module to a patient registry: address history capture, geocoding plan, derived variables | Design, tooling, governance guidance | Registry funders |
| Map trial site locations against the eligible population | Site access analysis | Trial networks, industry sponsors |

## Environmental context

| Idea | GeoALS role | Suits |
|---|---|---|
| Link residential history in an existing cohort to open environmental data sets | Linkage design and execution, documentation | Research programs on gene and environment interaction |
| Build a reusable, documented linkage pipeline that other cohorts can run | Pipeline development, open publication | Infrastructure and data science funders |
| Engage community members in describing places and concerns they want studied | Engagement, participatory mapping | Community-engaged research programs |

## Data standards and infrastructure

| Idea | GeoALS role | Suits |
|---|---|---|
| Represent location history and environmental context in a common data model, with a published implementation guide | Informatics lead or consultant | Informatics and data standards programs |
| Contribute term requests and use cases to open ontologies and working groups | Curation and community liaison | Open science funders |
| Publish GeoALS clinic and resource layers with full metadata, identifiers and licenses | Data stewardship | Open data and infrastructure funders |

## Advocacy and policy

| Idea | GeoALS role | Suits |
|---|---|---|
| Extend the Legislative Viewer to state legislatures | App development, data maintenance | Advocacy funders |
| Produce district fact sheets from Hub data for advocacy days | Automated map and fact sheet production | Advocacy organizations |

Organizations can also commission or co-create an accessible StoryMap with GeOALS. These interactive narratives can combine text, photographs, media and optional maps. See [StoryMaps consultation and creation](../work/services.md).

## Training and capacity

| Idea | GeoALS role | Suits |
|---|---|---|
| Expert-led training tailored to a research team, clinic or community organization | Needs assessment, curriculum, instruction, exercises and follow-up | Capacity-building funders; training components of research grants |
| A StoryMaps workshop that helps a partner explain care access, community resources or study findings | Story planning, hands-on instruction, accessibility and source review, feedback on a draft | Community engagement, education and dissemination funders |
| A short course on GIS for patient advocacy organizations, built on this site | Curriculum, instruction | Capacity-building funders |
| Fellowships or internships placing GIS students with the Mappers | Mentorship, project supply | Education and workforce programs |
| Accessible mapping: methods and tools for map makers who use eye-gaze and other assistive technology | Lived expertise, tool testing | Disability and assistive technology funders |

See [Training with GeOALS](training.md) for a topic menu, formats and an example StoryMaps workshop scope.

## Other neurodegenerative diseases

The clinic locator, clinic review and legislative mapping models can be adapted for another disease community. A partner organization would bring its clinic lists, community and priorities, and GeoALS its platform and method.
