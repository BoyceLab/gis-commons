# Budget and letters

Budget justification text and letter templates. Confirm rates, signers and organizational documents with GeoALS before using these templates. Follow the funder's own format and cost rules where they differ from the examples here, and use your institution's current figures for its own lines.

<p><a href="#budget-lines">Budget lines</a> · <a href="#budget-justification-text">Justification text</a> · <a href="#letter-of-commitment">Letter of commitment</a> · <a href="#letter-of-support">Letter of support</a> · <a href="#consultant-letter">Consultant letter</a></p>

## Budget lines

| Arrangement | Where it usually appears | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Subaward | Consortium or subaward costs | GeoALS personnel effort, consultants, other direct costs, and indirect costs at GeoALS's rate |
| Consultant | Consultant costs | Hours multiplied by an hourly rate |
| Fee-for-service | Other direct costs or purchased services | Fixed price per deliverable from a quote |
| Community engagement | Participant support or other direct costs | Reviewer compensation and coordination time |
| Expert-led training | Consultant, training services or subaward costs, depending on the agreement | Preparation, delivery and follow-up hours at an agreed rate, plus any approved materials or access costs |
| Software and hosting | Other direct costs, if needed | Confirm with GeoALS; budget project-specific software, credits or storage only if the scope requires them |

Use the indirect cost rate agreed for the application, subject to the funder's rules. See [Project arrangements](index.md#project-arrangements).

## Budget justification text

**Subaward**

> GeoALS (subaward, <span class="fill">[$ amount]</span> per year). GeoALS will perform <span class="fill">[scope]</span>. Costs include <span class="fill">[role, percent effort]</span> for project leadership and analysis; consultants from the GeoALS expert pool in <span class="fill">[areas]</span> for <span class="fill">[N]</span> hours at <span class="fill">[$ rate]</span> per hour; <span class="fill">[other direct costs]</span>; and indirect costs at GeoALS's rate.

**Consultant**

> GIS consultant, GeoALS expert pool (<span class="fill">[N]</span> hours at <span class="fill">[$ rate]</span> per hour, <span class="fill">[$ total]</span>). The consultant will <span class="fill">[tasks]</span>. The rate is set by GeoALS.

**Fee-for-service**

> Geospatial services, GeoALS (<span class="fill">[$ total]</span>). Fixed-price production of <span class="fill">[deliverables]</span>, per the attached quote. The price includes data preparation, analysis, cartography, one round of revision, and publication to <span class="fill">[the ALS Geospatial Hub / a private group]</span>.

**Community reviewers**

> Community reviewer compensation (<span class="fill">[N]</span> reviewers, <span class="fill">[N]</span> hours each, at <span class="fill">[$ rate]</span> per hour). People living with ALS and caregivers will review <span class="fill">[materials]</span>.

### Expert-led training

> GeOALS training (<span class="fill">[$ total]</span>). GeOALS experts will deliver <span class="fill">[N sessions / course]</span> on <span class="fill">[ArcGIS StoryMaps / GIS fundamentals / other topic]</span> for <span class="fill">[audience and group size]</span>. The budget covers <span class="fill">[N]</span> preparation hours, <span class="fill">[N]</span> instructional hours and <span class="fill">[N]</span> follow-up hours at <span class="fill">[$ agreed rate]</span> per hour, plus <span class="fill">[approved materials, accessibility or software costs, if needed]</span>. Deliverables include <span class="fill">[teaching materials, exercises and feedback on a draft product]</span>.

## Letter of commitment

For a subaward or other funded role. Obtain the letter from GeoALS on approved letterhead with an authorized signer.

> <span class="fill">[Date]</span>
>
> <span class="fill">[Principal Investigator name, title, institution]</span>
>
> Re: <span class="fill">[Project title]</span>, <span class="fill">[Funder and opportunity]</span>
>
> Dear <span class="fill">[Dr. Name]</span>:
>
> GeoALS is pleased to commit to participating in the project named above. GeoALS uses geographic information systems (GIS) to improve care, accelerate research, and advocate for the ALS community.
>
> If the project is funded, GeoALS will <span class="fill">[scope of work in two or three sentences, with deliverables]</span>. This work will be led at GeoALS by <span class="fill">[role]</span>, with support from consultants in the GeoALS expert pool. Our budget for this scope is <span class="fill">[$ amount]</span> over <span class="fill">[period]</span>, as detailed in the attached budget and justification.
>
> GeoALS will comply with the terms of the award and with the data protections described in the proposal. We look forward to working with you.
>
> Sincerely,
>
> <span class="fill">[Authorized official, title]</span>, GeoALS

## Letter of support

For an unfunded endorsement.

> <span class="fill">[Date]</span>
>
> Dear <span class="fill">[Dr. Name]</span>:
>
> I am writing on behalf of GeoALS in support of your proposal, <span class="fill">[title]</span>, to <span class="fill">[funder]</span>.
>
> <span class="fill">[One or two sentences on why the project is relevant to people living with ALS and to GeoALS's mission.]</span>
>
> GeoALS's public resources are available to your team, including the ALS Geospatial Hub, the ALS Clinic Advisor clinic database, and our map and story galleries. <span class="fill">[If applicable: We would be glad to share your findings with the ALS community through the Hub.]</span>
>
> We wish you success with this application.
>
> Sincerely,
>
> <span class="fill">[Name, title]</span>, GeoALS
