# StoryMaps

Story maps combine narrative, maps and media to explain how geography connects to people's lives. These published ArcGIS StoryMaps explore ALS care and advocacy. Open a story below to read it here, or follow its full-page link for more room to navigate the maps.

## Patterns of Care in the U.S.

Pat Dolan examines access to ALS care through clinic locations, travel time and barriers such as transportation and internet access. The story also introduces the ALS Clinic Advisor and perspectives from people living with ALS and caregivers.

## More stories and ways to use them

- Browse the [ALS Geospatial Hub story gallery](https://als-geospatial-hub-nonprofit.hub.arcgis.com/pages/the-stories).
- Pair a story with a [video walkthrough](videos.md) or the [GIS introduction](../start/gis-basics.md).
- For an environmental research story, start with an [exposure example](../environment/examples.md), explain the place and time represented, and distinguish a mapped exposure indicator from an individual's measured exposure. Include the original references and the study's limits.
- Learn about [ArcGIS StoryMaps](https://www.esri.com/en-us/arcgis/products/arcgis-storymaps/overview) and [crediting maps and data](../esri/credits.md).
